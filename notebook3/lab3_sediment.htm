Data Notes

The sediment observations shown here are adapted from the SERC/GEODE Surficial Sea Floor Sediment Map, which compiles data from DSDP, ODP, IODP, WHOI, and related marine sample sources. The original Google Earth marine sediment database was developed by Kristen St. John, with assistance from students at James Madison University, as part of the NSF-funded GEODE project. For more please see Exploring Marine Sediments Using Google Earth.
